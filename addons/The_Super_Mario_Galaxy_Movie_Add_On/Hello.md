# **The Super Mario Galaxy Movie Add-on**
## Creating Circuits. in Minecraft.

Welcome to the Super Mario Galaxy Movie Add-on for Minecraft! With this mod, you can place your Super Mario Galaxy Movie characters, inside your Minecraft world! (For the best experience, make it a 3D map. Thank you!)

## Our characters you can place
**Lakitu** Lakitu is a BIG NEMESIS which hurls projectiles at you. It flys out of reach, so be careful.

**Hammer Bro** The Hammer Brothers are Koopas that hurls high damage hammers at you. try to avoid the projectiles.

**Bowser** The boss of all time. it shoots fireballs so be careful near him.

**Bowser Jr.** He is Bowser's son, and he specifically possesses mace or a paintbrush. most likely a mace. But don't worry, it deals same damage no matter what. He is another boss.

**Yoshi** He is unarguably the only passive mob in this add on. Luigi has to be, too,  but his head is too big to achieve that. Sometimea he does not cooperate with your riding. That's okay.
